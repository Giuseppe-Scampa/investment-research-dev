# Governance and roadmap — public synthetic research demo

## Current capabilities

- Strict CSV and identity validation; exploration vs shortlist vs pre-trade readiness.
- Sample 2026 fiscal rules, calendar, distinct taxable events and simulated offset of prior losses.
- Explicit price/cost uncertainty and alternative A/B/C demonstrations with a decision ledger.
- Public research snapshots; financial data are not live executable market quotes.

## Risks and limits

1. Tax rules are illustrative, with unknown classification for some product types and no per-ISIN broker confirmation.
2. Fees and foreign-exchange assumptions are invented test parameters, never actual client pricing.
3. Some market/session calendars are 2026-specific and cannot be assumed correct for 2027.
4. Bid/ask, depth, contemporaneous FX and personal fiscal-wallet balances are unavailable.
5. Historical scenarios are not predictive backtests; sparse free datasets have coverage limits.
6. No live brokerage connection, automated trading or human decision has occurred.
7. Downloaded market facts and external source URLs must be verified at publication and execution time.

## Next phases

- Data-provider/cache/source-verification and global screening, without a paywall dependency.
- Interpretable ranking, stress analysis, macro-event register, and clear reason-codes.
- Optional free-tier AI-assisted document summaries with hard resource ceilings and source validation.
